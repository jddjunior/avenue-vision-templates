What is the difference between a deep clean and a regular clean?

A regular visit resets the kitchen, baths, floors and dusting to reach. A deep clean adds baseboards, door frames, blinds, vents, fan blades and behind the appliances. It takes about twice as long.

Do I need to be home while you clean?

No. Most regulars leave a key or a door code. We text when we arrive and again when we lock up, and the same 2 people come every time.
