Will It Fit My Toilet?

Three questions, and we tell you what fits and what needs fixing first.

1. What shape is your toilet bowl?

Seat bolts to front rim: about 16.5 in is round, 18.5 in is elongated.

2. Is there a GFCI outlet within 4 ft of the toilet?
3. How is the shutoff valve behind the toilet?

Your result

Good fit. A seat, attachment or smart toilet all work.

  • Elongated bowl: pick an elongated seat.
  • Outlet nearby: powered seats work.
  • Valve turns easily: we add a T-valve, no wall work.

Bidet Questions from Orlando Homeowners

How much does it cost to install a bidet in Orlando?

Most installs run $[LOW]–$[HIGH]. Attachments cost least, smart toilets most, since the whole toilet is replaced. You see the full price first.

Can a bidet seat go on any toilet?

Most toilets take one if the seat matches the bowl shape. Some skirted and wall-hung toilets need a specific model, so we check yours first.

Do I need an electrician for an electric bidet seat?

Only if there is no GFCI outlet within about 4 ft. If there is, we plug in and route the cord neatly. If not, pick a non-electric attachment or add an outlet first.

Does Orlando water clog bidet nozzles?

Central Florida water scales up nozzles and inlet screens over time. Most weak-spray calls are a blocked screen, not a broken seat. An inline filter ($[PRICE]) helps.
