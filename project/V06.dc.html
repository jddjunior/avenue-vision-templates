Will insurance pay for hurricane roof damage in Florida?

Often, when wind or a named storm caused the damage. We photograph it, write it up and meet your adjuster on the roof. Your policy and hurricane deductible decide what is covered.

What is a wind mitigation inspection, and do I need one?

It is the state form (OIR-B1-1802) that records your roof deck, straps and shape. Many Florida insurers lower windstorm premiums when it is on file. We complete it for $[PRICE], or free with a new roof.
