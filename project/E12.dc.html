QUESTION 02

Can my panel handle a Level 2 EV charger?

Often, yes. A Level 2 charger needs its own 240 V circuit, usually 40 to 60 A. We run a load calculation first. If the panel is full, a load-sharing charger or an upgrade solves it.

More on /ev-charger-installation

QUESTION 03

Do I need a permit for electrical work in Hillsborough County?

For panels, new circuits, generators and EV chargers, yes. Swapping a light fixture or a switch usually does not. We pull the permit, book the inspection and meet the inspector.

Good Sparks · (813) 555-0172

QUESTION 04

What size generator does a Brandon house need?

To keep central AC running through a summer outage, most 3-bed homes need a 20 to 24 kW standby unit. A portable generator with an inlet and interlock covers the fridge, lights and fans for far less.
