Do I need a permit for a fence in Indian River County?

For most residential fences, yes, and many HOAs want their own approval too. We pull the county or city permit and send the HOA packet with your survey. It shows on the price sheet as one line: $[PRICE].

Aluminum and vinyl. Salt air and sprinklers gray wood within a couple of seasons. Pressure-treated pine still lasts [YEARS] years with a sealer. On the barrier island we use stainless hardware and say so on the quote.
