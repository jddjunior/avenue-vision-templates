How much does garage door spring repair cost in Panama City?

Transcript

Most two-car doors here run on a pair of torsion springs, and we replace both so they wear out together. In Bay County that typically lands at $[LOW]–$[HIGH] with parts, labor and a balance test. Text a photo of the springs and we'll tell you the size.

0:37

Can I open my garage door with a broken spring?

Transcript

Please don't. A two-car steel door can weigh 150 pounds or more, and the spring is what makes it feel light. With the spring gone, the opener tries to lift all of it and can strip a gear. Leave the door closed, unplug the opener and send us a photo.

0:52

Do I need a hurricane-rated garage door in Panama City?

Transcript

If you're replacing the door, yes. A new door in Bay County needs a permit and a wind-load rating that matches your home's design pressure. We pull the permit and leave the rating label on for the inspector. Older doors can take a brace kit.

0:33

How long does a garage door opener last?

Transcript

Most of the chain and belt openers we replace are 10 to 15 years old. If yours is younger than that and the motor hums but won't move, it's usually a $[PRICE] gear kit or a logic board, not a whole new opener. We'll tell you which before we touch it.
