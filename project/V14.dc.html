Lakeland Lawn Questions, Answered

When should I fertilize my lawn in Lakeland?

Feed St. Augustine from late February through May and again in October. In summer we switch to iron and potassium with no nitrogen or phosphorus where local rules require.

Why is my St. Augustine grass turning brown in summer?

In Polk County it is often chinch bugs, which hit sunny, dry spots by the driveway first. Large patch fungus shows up in cooler, wet months as rings. We check for both every round.
