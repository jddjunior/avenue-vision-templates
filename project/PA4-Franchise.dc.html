Who we look for

We Can Teach the Squeegee. We Can't Teach Character.

Most Clearday owners came from outside the trade: [PRIOR FIELDS]. Window cleaning experience is not required. How you treat people is.

  1. 01 You keep your word on small things Arriving inside the window, calling back the same day. Customers notice, and so does your crew.
  2. 02 You would rather lead than go it alone Year one you may be on the ladder. The goal is to run the team that is on it.
  3. 03 You like being known around town Vans, yard signs and local events grow a Clearday. You are the face of it.
  4. 04 You will run the system The playbook works when it is followed. Bring ideas; good ones get shared with every crew.
