How much does an electrician cost in St. Petersburg?

Small repairs run $[LOW]–$[HIGH]. The trip fee is $[PRICE] and comes off the bill if you approve the work. Panel upgrades and EV chargers get a written price after we see the panel.

Do I need a permit for a panel upgrade?

Yes. A panel change in St. Petersburg needs a permit and an inspection. We pull the permit, book the inspector and coordinate the power shutoff with the utility.

Can my panel handle an EV charger?

Most 200-amp panels can. A 100-amp panel in an older Kenwood or Old Northeast home often needs a load calculation first. We run it on the first visit and tell you which one you have.

What does a 4-point inspection check on the electrical?

Insurers look at the panel brand and age, the wire type and any double-tapped breakers. Federal Pacific and Zinsco panels are flagged by most Florida insurers. We inspect and, if needed, price the fix in the same visit.
