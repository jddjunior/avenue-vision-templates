Northstar Club Questions From Tampa Homeowners

Can I cancel the Northstar Club anytime?

Yes. Call or email us. Monthly plans stop at the next billing date with no fee. Yearly plans: [REFUND TERMS].

What does the yearly electrical inspection cover?

Panel and breakers, GFCI and AFCI protection, smoke and CO alarms, surge protection, grounding, outdoor and pool circuits, and your generator hookup. You get a report with photos.

Can renters join the club?

Yes. The inspection and priority booking are yours. Repairs to the home need your landlord's OK, or your landlord can hold the plan for the unit.
