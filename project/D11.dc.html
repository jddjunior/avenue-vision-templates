Questions

What people ask before they replace

How much does a new AC cost in Fort Myers?

Most single-story homes here land between $[LOW] and $[HIGH] installed for a matched condenser and air handler. Size, efficiency and where the air handler sits move the number. The chat above gives you your range in about two minutes.

How long does an AC last in Southwest Florida?

Plan on 10 to 15 years. Our systems run most of the year, and salt air near the canals and the beach wears outdoor coils faster than inland units in Lehigh Acres.

Should I repair or replace my AC?

If the system is over 12 years old and the repair costs more than a third of a new one, replacing usually wins. If it is younger, we will tell you to fix it and give you the repair price first.

Do I need a permit to replace an AC in Lee County?

Yes. A changeout needs a mechanical permit from Lee County or your city, and an inspector checks the work. We pull it, schedule the inspection, and the fee is already in your price.
