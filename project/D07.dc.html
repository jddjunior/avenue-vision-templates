Questions

The ones we get at the door

Q.01

How much does it cost to coat a 2-car garage floor in Fort Lauderdale?

A typical 2-car garage here is about 450 square feet. A full flake floor with a polyaspartic topcoat runs $[LOW]–$[HIGH] installed, and that includes grinding and normal crack repair. We measure and give you the number in writing at the visit.

Q.02

Is polyaspartic better than epoxy for a garage in Florida?

For the topcoat, yes in most garages. Polyaspartic is UV stable, so it does not yellow when the door is open, and it cures in hours instead of days. We still use epoxy underneath on slabs that need more build or a moisture primer.

Q.03

How long before I can park on a new garage floor coating?

You can walk on it about 6 hours after the topcoat goes down and park a car on it in 24 hours. Give it 48 hours before a lift, jack stands or a heavy truck. In a cold snap we will tell you to wait longer.

Q.04

Will a garage floor coating peel from moisture in the slab?

It can if nobody checks. We meter every slab before we quote. If it reads damp, which happens near the canals and the beach, we put down a moisture-tolerant primer first and include that line in your written price.
