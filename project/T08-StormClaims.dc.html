Insurance claims, start to finish

How a Roof Insurance Claim Works in Sarasota

Five steps. Each one says what you do and what we do, so nobody waits on the wrong person.

  1. 01

    Inspection and photo report

    What you do

    Book a time. Be home, or leave the side gate open.

    What we do

    Walk every slope, take dated photos and send a written report within 24 hours.

  2. 02

    You file with your insurer

    What you do

    Call your insurer or file online. Attach our report if you choose.

    What we do

    Answer your questions about what we found. The claim is yours to file.

  3. 03

    We meet the adjuster

    What you do

    Set the adjuster visit and text us the day and time.

    What we do

    Walk the roof with the adjuster and show each finding from the report.

  4. 04

    Approved scope

    What you do

    Share the approved scope. Pick your materials and colors.

    What we do

    Check the scope against our report and flag anything missing, so you can ask your insurer.

  5. 05

    Build and final photos

    What you do

    Pay your deductible and approve the start date.

    What we do

    Pull the permit, build the roof and send before-and-after photos for your records.

We are roofers, not public adjusters. We document damage and explain our estimate. We do not negotiate your claim; that stays between you and your insurer.

Florida notice: You are responsible for paying any insurance deductible. It is insurance fraud punishable as a third-degree felony for a contractor to knowingly or willfully, and with intent to injure, defraud or deceive, pay, waive or rebate all or part of an insurance deductible applicable to payment to the contractor for repairs to a property covered by a property insurance policy. You may file a complaint with the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation regarding any contractor violation.
