Fence Questions From Polk County Homeowners

Do I need a permit for a fence in Polk County?

In most cases, yes. The City of Lakeland and Polk County each set their own rules for height and setbacks. We check which rules apply to your address, file the permit and put the fee in your written quote.

Will my HOA approve a new fence?

Most HOAs want a drawing, the material and the height first. We hand you a site sketch and product sheet for your HOA form, and we do not set a post until it is approved.

Do I need a survey to build on my property line?

It helps. If you have the survey from your closing, we measure from it. If the line is unclear, we point you to a licensed surveyor before we dig. We never guess at a property line.
