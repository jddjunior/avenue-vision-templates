Field questions

The four questions we hear most at the door in Leon County, answered the way we answer them on the lot.

Q.01

How much does tree removal cost in Tallahassee?

Most removals we quote in Leon County land between $[LOW] and $[HIGH]. The number turns on height, lean, what sits under the tree and whether a bucket truck or crane can reach it. Stump grinding is quoted on its own line, from $[PRICE] per stump.

Q.02

Do I need a permit to cut down a tree in Tallahassee?

Sometimes. The City of Tallahassee and Leon County both protect certain trees, and large live oaks are the usual case. We look up your parcel before the walkthrough and tell you at the door whether a permit applies and who files it.

Q.03

When is the best time to trim oak trees in North Florida?

November through February. The oaks are dormant, the cuts close as spring growth starts, and the crown is light before hurricane season opens on June 1. Dead or hanging limbs come out any month.

Q.04

Does homeowners insurance pay for tree removal after a storm?

Usually, when the tree lands on a covered structure: the house, a garage, a fence. A tree that falls in open yard often is not covered. We photograph the damage, write an itemized invoice your adjuster can read and can meet the adjuster on site.
