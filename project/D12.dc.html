How much does lawn care cost in Lakeland?

02

When should I fertilize my lawn in Central Florida?

St. Augustine and Bahia lawns in Polk County green up in March. We start feeding then and space applications through the growing season, and we follow any local summer limits on nitrogen.

03

How short should I cut St. Augustine grass?

3.5 to 4 inches for most St. Augustine. Cutting lower stresses it in Florida heat and opens the door to chinch bugs and weeds. We log the deck height on every visit so you can check.

04

What are the brown patches in my lawn?

In summer it is usually chinch bugs, which hit the hot, sunny edges by the driveway and sidewalk first. In cooler, wet months it is often large patch fungus. We confirm with a can test or a soil plug before we treat anything.
