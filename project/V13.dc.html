Can you remove bats from my house in summer?

Not from April 15 to August 14. That is bat maternity season in Florida and state rules bar exclusion while pups cannot fly. We inspect and seal everything else, then exclude the colony once the season ends.

What is making noise in my attic at night?

Quick scratching after dark is usually roof rats. Heavy thumps at dusk point to raccoons, and noise at sunrise points to squirrels. We confirm with droppings and tracks during the inspection.

Does homeowners insurance cover wildlife damage?

Some policies cover damage from raccoons or squirrels but not from rodents. We photograph every entry point and repair so you have what you need to file a claim.
