(Q)uestions.

What Orlando people ask before they book

How much does house cleaning cost in Orlando?

A standard clean of a 3-bed, 2-bath home runs $[LOW]–$[HIGH]. Deep cleans and move-outs cost more because they take about twice as long. You get the number by text before we book.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No. Many clients leave a door code or a lockbox. We text when we arrive and again when we lock up.

What is the difference between a standard and a deep clean?

A deep clean adds the slow work: baseboards by hand, inside the oven and fridge, grout lines and window tracks. Book one first, then switch to standard visits.

Do house cleaners bring their own supplies?

Ours do. Vacuums, mops, cloths and products come with the crew. Want fragrance-free, or your own products used? Tell us when you book.
