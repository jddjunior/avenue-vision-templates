The bench

One crew, from tear-out to grout

Your bathroom does not get handed between three subcontractors. The same two or three people pull the old tile, flatten the floor, waterproof, set and grout. A walk-in shower takes five working days, and here is how they go.

  1. Day 0Measure. Moisture and flatness readings, photos, a tile count with 10% spare.
  2. Day 1Tear-out and prep. Old tile and mortar out, subfloor checked to 1/8 in. in 10 ft.
  3. Day 2Waterproofing. Membrane on every wet wall and the pan, then a 24-hour flood test.
  4. Days 3–4Setting. Layout snapped from the center line so cuts land evenly at both walls.
  5. Day 5Grout, caulk, seal. We walk it with you before the tools go back in the van.

Questions at the door

What people ask before we measure

Q.01

How much does tile installation cost in St. Augustine?

Labor for most floor and wall tile runs $[LOW]–$[HIGH] per square foot here, before tile. A standard 5 ft tub surround, torn out, waterproofed and retiled, lands around $[LOW]–$[HIGH]. You get one written price after the measure.

Q.02

How long does it take to retile a shower?

Five working days for most walk-in showers: one for tear-out, one for waterproofing and the flood test, two for setting, one for grout and sealing. You can shower 72 hours after the grout goes in.

Q.03

Can you put new tile over old tile?

On a floor, sometimes. If the old tile is flat, fully bonded and the floor does not flex, we can set over it with a bonding primer and save a day. In showers we always tear out, because we will not vouch for what is behind it.

Q.04

Do you install coquina or travertine pool decks?

Yes. Pavers go down on a compacted base or over sound concrete, mortar-set or sand-set. A 400 sq ft pool deck takes 4 to 6 days and runs from $[PRICE] per square foot installed.
