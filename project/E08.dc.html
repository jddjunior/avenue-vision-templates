Will solar panels lower my power bill in Palm Bay?

It depends on your roof, your shade and how much power you use. Some homes see a big drop, some a small one, and a few aren't a fit. We run your last 12 bills through the math and hand you a statement like the example above. If the net line doesn't help you, we say so and leave it there.

²

How much do solar panels cost in Palm Bay?

Most Palm Bay homes we quote land between $[LOW] and $[HIGH] for a [SIZE] kW system before any incentive. Price moves with roof size, panel count, battery choice and how far your electrical panel sits from the array. Every line is on your written proposal.

³

Do solar panels hold up in a Florida hurricane?

The panels and racking we install are rated for [WIND RATING] mph winds and attached to Florida Building Code for your roof. We seal and photograph every roof attachment for your file. After a named storm, we offer a storm check from $[PRICE].

⁴

Do solar panels work when the power goes out?

Not on their own. Grid-tied systems shut off in an outage by design, so line crews stay safe. To keep the lights and fridge on, you need a battery. We show it as its own line on your statement, so you can decide with the number in front of you.
