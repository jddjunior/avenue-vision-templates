Home Cleaning Questions From Florida Homeowners

How much does house cleaning cost in Florida?

Each Tidewell prices for its own area. A recurring clean of a typical home runs $[LOW]–$[HIGH], and your location gives you a set price before the first visit.

Will I get the same house cleaner every time?

Yes. You get one team lead by name. If they are out, your location tells you who is coming before the day of your clean.

Do house cleaners bring their own supplies?

Yes. Every team brings its own supplies and equipment. If you want us to use your products, tell your location when you book.

Is each Tidewell location locally owned?

Yes. Every location is independently owned and operated by someone who lives in the area, under the same Tidewell standards.
