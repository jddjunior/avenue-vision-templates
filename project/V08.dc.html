Naples painting questions

How much does it cost to paint a house exterior in Naples?

Most single-story stucco homes here run $[LOW]–$[HIGH]. Two stories, tile-roof cutting-in and heavy crack repair push it higher. Your written quote lists every line.

How often should you paint a house exterior in Florida?

Every 7 to 10 years for stucco in Southwest Florida sun and salt air, sooner on wood trim and south-facing walls. Fading, chalky residue and hairline cracks are the signs.

Do you handle HOA color approval for condos?

Yes. We pull your association's approved palette, prepare the submittal with color codes and wait for approval before we start.

How long does cabinet painting take?

Most kitchens take 4 to 6 working days. Doors go to our shop for spraying, so your kitchen stays usable for most of the job.
