St. Pete Window Cleaning Questions

How often should St. Petersburg windows be cleaned with the salt air?

Near the bay or the Gulf, salt film builds up fast, so most of those homes go quarterly. Homes farther inland usually do fine twice a year. After the first visit we'll suggest a schedule based on what we saw on your glass.

Will rain ruin my windows right after a cleaning?

No. Rain spots are mostly the dirt that was already on the glass, and clean glass sheds rain with little left behind. If spots show up within [DAYS] days of our visit, call us and we'll redo that glass at no charge.

Do you clean the inside of the windows too?

Yes. Inside glass, frames and sills are part of every full service, with shoe covers on in the house and towels under each window. Want outside only? Tell us when you ask for your quote and we'll price it that way.

Can you get hard water spots off my windows?

Light sprinkler spotting usually comes off in a standard visit. Heavy mineral buildup takes a separate restoration treatment at $[PRICE] per pane. We test one pane first so you see the result before you decide on the rest.
