How a remodel runs, start to keys

Four stages, one team. You see a drawing and a fixed number before anything is torn out, and the number does not move unless you change the plan.

  1. Stage oneMeasure

    We visit, measure every wall and ask how you live in the room. The first visit is free and takes about 90 minutes.

  2. Stage twoDesign

    Floor plans, elevations and a materials board, revised until it is right. Design fee from $[PRICE], credited to the build.

  3. Stage threePrice

    One fixed price in writing, with permits and City of Winter Park or Orange County fees included.

  4. Stage fourBuild

    One project lead on site, a written schedule, and a walk-through with you every week until the punch list is done.

What does design-build mean, and why use it?

One company designs the room, prices it and builds it. You do not hire an architect and a contractor separately and referee between them. One contract, one schedule, one person to call.

3.

Do I need a permit to remodel in Winter Park?

Moving walls, plumbing, wiring or windows needs a City of Winter Park building permit, and homes in a historic district may also need design review. We draw, file and track every permit, and the fees are in your price.
