Questions

Asked most often at the first visit

Q. 1

How much does a custom pool cost in Naples?

Most new pools we build in Collier County land between $[LOW] and $[HIGH] with deck and screen enclosure. A plunge pool starts near $[PRICE]. You get one written price after the site visit, line by line, and it does not move unless the drawings do.

Q. 2

How long does it take to build a pool in Naples?

Plan on 14 to 20 weeks from signed drawings: 3 to 6 weeks for the permit, then 10 to 14 weeks on site. Summer storms can add a few days, and we tell you the week they do.

Q. 3

Do I need a permit for a pool in Collier County?

Yes. Every in-ground pool, spa and screen enclosure needs a building permit from Collier County, the City of Naples or Marco Island, plus a safety barrier that meets Florida's pool safety law. We file it, book the inspections and close it out.

Q. 4

Can you renovate a pool you did not build?

Yes. A resurface with new waterline tile and coping takes 4 to 7 weeks and runs $[LOW]–$[HIGH] for a typical Naples pool. We check the shell, plumbing and equipment first and tell you what can stay.
