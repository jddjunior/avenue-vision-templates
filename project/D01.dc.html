Four questions Tampa homeowners ask before they call

How much does a plumber cost in Tampa?

Most repairs land between $[LOW] and $[HIGH]. The $[PRICE] trip fee covers finding the problem and comes off the bill if you approve the work. The price is written down before work starts.

Can a plumber come today for a leak?

Usually. Call before [TIME] and a truck reaches most of Hillsborough County the same day. If water is running, close the main valve at the meter or where the line enters the house.

How do I know if I have a slab leak?

Three signs: a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water with every tap off, and a bill that jumps with no change in use. Shut every fixture and watch the meter for 15 minutes. If the dial moves, call.

Should I repair or replace a 12-year-old water heater?

With Tampa's hard water, most tank heaters last 8 to 12 years. At 12, with rust in the water or a puddle at the base, replacing usually costs less than the next round of repairs. We write both prices so you can compare.
