What Jacksonville homeowners ask us

Q.01

How much does pest control cost in Jacksonville?

Quarterly general pest service runs $[LOW]–$[HIGH] per visit for most homes under 3,000 sq ft. The first visit costs more because we treat inside and out. Termite and bed bug work is quoted after an inspection, in writing.

Q.02

How often should I spray for bugs in Florida?

Every three months for most Jacksonville homes. Heat and humidity keep roaches and ants active all year, so a quarterly perimeter band is the standard. Mosquito yard treatments run monthly from March to November.

Q.03

When do termites swarm in Jacksonville?

Native subterranean termites swarm on warm days after rain, February to May. Formosan termites swarm on late-spring evenings near lights. Wings on a windowsill mean call us; save a few in a bag.

Q.04

Is pest control safe for kids and pets?

Yes, once the product dries, usually 30 to 60 minutes. After each visit we text what we applied, where, and how long to keep pets off treated areas.
