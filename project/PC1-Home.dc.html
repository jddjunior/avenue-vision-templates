24/7 emergency service

Is It a Plumbing Emergency? Call Right Away If

  • I have water coming through a ceiling, wall or floor
  • I have a burst or split pipe
  • I have sewage coming up a tub, shower or floor drain
  • I have a water heater leaking from the tank
Call Now (888) 555-0150 routes you to the nearest open location

What to do in the next five minutes

  1. 1Shut off the main water valve. It is usually where the line enters the house, or in a box at the street by the meter. Turn it clockwise.
  2. 2Turn off the water heater. Electric: switch off its breaker. Gas: set the dial to pilot.
  3. 3Keep water away from outlets. Unplug what you can reach safely and move rugs, boxes and electronics.
  4. 4Call us and take photos. Photos help your insurer. We answer around the clock.

Smell gas? Leave the house first. Call your gas company or 911 from outside, then call us.
