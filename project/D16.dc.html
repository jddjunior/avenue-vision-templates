Before you pourQuestions we get at the curb

Straight answers for Pensacola heat, sand and hurricane rain.

Q1

How much does a concrete driveway cost in Pensacola?

Most 4-inch broom-finish driveways we pour run $[LOW]–$[HIGH] per square foot with tear-out. Rebar or fiber, and saw-cut joints every 10 feet or less, are in that price, not extras.

Q2

How long before I can drive on new concrete?

Walk on it after 24 hours. Park a car on it after 7 days. Wait 28 days for a loaded truck or a boat trailer. In summer we wet-cure or spray a curing compound the same afternoon so it does not crack early.

Q3

Do I need a permit for a driveway in Escambia County?

Usually, when the drive meets the county right-of-way or you widen the curb cut. Inside Pensacola city limits the city handles it. We pull the permit and book the inspection, and the fee is its own line on your quote.

Q4

Concrete or pavers for a pool deck in Florida?

Concrete costs less up front and has no joints for weeds or ants. Pavers cost more but lift out for repairs. For pool decks we pour with a textured cool-deck or stamped finish so it stays walkable barefoot in August.
