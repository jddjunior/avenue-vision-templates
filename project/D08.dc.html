Questions we answer on the phone every week

4.1

How much does it cost to replace a water heater in Gainesville?

A standard 40 or 50 gallon tank swap runs $[LOW]–$[HIGH] installed. That includes hauling the old one away, a new T&P valve, a pan, an expansion tank where code calls for it, and the permit. Tankless starts at $[PRICE].

4.2

Is a tankless water heater worth it?

If you have gas and the house runs showers back to back, usually yes. On an all-electric house, a heat pump water heater saves more per dollar here, because a Florida garage keeps it warm most of the year.

4.3

Do I need a water softener in Gainesville?

Test first. On a private well the answer is often yes. On city water it often is not. The water test is free, takes 20 minutes and you keep the numbers whether you buy anything or not.

4.4

Why does my hot water smell like rotten eggs?

Usually bacteria in the tank reacting with the magnesium anode rod. Swapping it for an aluminum-zinc or powered anode fixes most cases. If the cold water smells too, the source is the well, and the fix is a filter like the WF-10.
