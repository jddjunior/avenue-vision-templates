Unit 2How a visit works

Four lessons, one visit, one report card.

  1. 1
    Lesson 1 · 15 min

    Walk the whole yard

    We find where they breed and where they rest: bromeliads, gutters, plant saucers, the shady side of the hedge.

  2. 2
    Lesson 2 · 10 min

    Tip and toss

    Standing water gets dumped or flushed. One bottle cap of water is enough for a batch of eggs.

  3. 3
    Lesson 3 · 30 min

    Treat the resting spots

    Mosquitoes spend the day under leaves. We treat the undersides of shrubs and the lanai edges, never open flowers.

  4. 4
    Lesson 4 · 5 min

    Hand over the report card

    You get grades for each part of the yard and what to do before we come back.

QUESTION 2

How long does a mosquito yard treatment last?

2 or 3 daysAbout 3 to 4 weeksAll summer

Answer: About 21 to 30 days on leaves that stay put. Heavy summer rain shortens that, which is why the plan comes back every month from June to October.

QUESTION 3

Is mosquito spray safe for kids, dogs and the pool?

Stay inside all dayOff the grass until dry

Answer: We use EPA-registered products at label rates and tell you which one before we spray. Keep kids and pets off treated areas until they dry, usually about 30 minutes. We never spray pool water, ponds or open flowers.

QUESTION 4

Why do I still get no-see-ums near the Intracoastal?

Bad screens onlyThey breed off your lot

Answer: No-see-ums breed in wet sand and mangrove mud, then drift in on calm evenings. Treating screens, lanai edges and nearby shrubs cuts them down. A ceiling fan on the lanai helps more than you would think.
