How much does carpet cleaning cost in Daytona Beach?

Most rooms run $[LOW]–$[HIGH] with pre-spray, a hot-water rinse and grooming. A 3-bedroom home with a hall usually lands at $[LOW]–$[HIGH]. Stairs, rugs and pet treatment go on the same written quote.

How long does carpet take to dry after cleaning?

Usually [HOURS] hours with the windows shut and the AC running, which pulls the moisture out. We leave air movers on the wettest rooms and set furniture legs on blocks.

Can you get dog and cat urine out of carpet?

Often, yes. A UV light shows every spot and the enzyme goes down to the pad. If urine has soaked the pad for months, we say so before we start, because a pad patch may be the real fix.

Do you clean area rugs in my home or take them?

Synthetic rugs can be cleaned in place. Wool and hand-knotted rugs go out for a full wash, dried flat and returned in [DAYS] days.
