How an exterior repaint goes

House recipe No. 02

The Delray Repaint

serves one house, about 2,000 sq ft

Ingredients

  • 1 pressure wash, 24 hours to dry
  • Every crack routed and patched
  • 1 coat masonry primer on chalky stucco
  • 2 coats exterior acrylic, walls and trim
  • 1 bead caulk at each window and door

Method

  1. 1Wash on day one. Plants rinsed before and after.
  2. 2Patch, caulk and prime once the walls are dry.
  3. 3First coat on the walls, then the trim.
  4. 4Second coat. We stop when the afternoon rain rolls in.
  5. 5Walk it with you and touch up on the spot.

Read before opening

The back label

Four questions Delray asks before the first coat.

  1. How much does it cost to paint a house exterior in Delray Beach?

    Most one-story block homes run $[LOW]–$[HIGH] for the wash, crack repair, primer where needed and two coats. Two stories, tall entries and lots of trim move it up. The written number comes after a 20-minute walk-around.

  2. How long does exterior paint last in South Florida?

    Plan on 7 to 10 years on stucco with good prep and two coats. Ocean-facing walls in Highland Beach and Ocean Ridge fade first; north walls last longest.

  3. Can you paint during the summer rainy season?

    Yes, on a schedule built around the storms. We start early, stop when the afternoon rain rolls in and never coat a wall that is still wet from the morning wash.

  4. Do I have to leave the house for interior painting?

    No. We paint one or two rooms at a time, keep a path clear, use low-odor paint and put the furniture back the same day.
