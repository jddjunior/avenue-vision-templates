How a St. Pete Paint Job Runs, Start to Finish

  1. 01

    Walkthrough and color consult

    We walk the house with you, note every repair, and test colors on your own walls.

  2. 02

    Written quote

    One price in writing, with prep, products and coats spelled out. No work until you sign.

  3. 03

    Prep and paint

    Wash, scrape, caulk and repair first. Then paint, with the site cleaned up every day.

  4. 04

    Final walkthrough with photos

    We walk it together and fix anything you flag. With your OK, the job gets its pin on our map.

Painting Questions From St. Pete Homeowners

How much does it cost to paint a house exterior in St. Petersburg?

Most single-story homes in St. Pete run $[LOW]–$[HIGH] for a full exterior. Stucco repair, wood rot and two-story access change the price. The walkthrough is free and the quote is in writing.

How long does exterior paint last in Florida?

With good prep and a quality exterior paint, plan on about [YEARS] years on stucco. South and west walls fade first, so we check those at the final walkthrough.

Can you paint around summer afternoon storms?

Yes. We start exterior work early and plan around the storms that roll in most summer afternoons. Paint needs a few dry hours to set, so we watch the radar and stop before rain.

Do you help pick colors that pass my HOA or historic district?

Yes. Every estimate includes a color consult. We test large samples on your walls and match them to your HOA palette or the historic district guidelines in neighborhoods like Kenwood and Old Northeast.
