Does homeowners insurance cover a burst pipe?

Sudden leaks, like a burst supply line, are usually covered. Slow leaks and flood water usually are not. Your policy decides, so we document everything and read the policy with you.

Is it safe to stay in my house with mold?

Small patches under 10 square feet can often be sealed off while you stay. Larger growth, or anyone with asthma at home, changes that. Florida requires a licensed remediator above 10 square feet.
