  2. Pool quizCard 2 of 4Q2

    How often should a pool be cleaned in Florida?

    Once a week, all year. Summer heat and afternoon storms burn off chlorine fast, and a pool skipped for two weeks in August can turn green. In January most pools still need the weekly brush and basket check.

    Collect all 4 · Cannonball
  3. Pool quizCard 3 of 4Q3

    How long does it take to clear a green pool?

    Most green pools are blue again in 3 to 5 days: shock, brush, run the filter around the clock and clean the filter twice. A black-green pool with a foot of leaves takes longer, and we tell you that on day one.
