How much does it cost to pressure wash a driveway in Sarasota?

A standard 2-car driveway runs $[LOW]–$[HIGH]. Pavers that need re-sanding or sealing are priced on their own, from $[PRICE] per 100 sq ft. You get the number in writing before we start.

Is pressure washing safe for a shingle or tile roof?

High pressure isn't. Roofs get a soft wash: low pressure under 500 PSI and a cleaning solution that kills the black algae streaks at the root, so they stay off longer. Shingle makers warn against high-pressure washing for this reason.

How often should I clean windows near the beach?

Gulf-facing glass on Siesta Key and Longboat Key collects salt film fast, so most owners book every 3 months. Inland homes in Lakewood Ranch and North Port usually book twice a year.

Do I need to be home during a pressure washing visit?

Not for outdoor work, as long as the gate is open and we can reach a spigot. Yes for inside windows. We text when we're 30 minutes out and again when we finish.
