Q/01 Cost

How much does junk removal cost in Miami?

Most pickups land between $[LOW] and $[HIGH]. Single items start at $[PRICE], and a full truck runs $[LOW]–$[HIGH]. Loading, hauling and dump fees are included, and you get the number in writing before we lift anything.

Q/02 Timing

Can you pick up junk today?

Usually. Call (305) 555-0143 before [CUTOFF TIME] and we can be there the same afternoon in most of Miami-Dade. After that, we book a two-hour arrival window for the next morning.

Q/03 Access

Do I need to be home for the pickup?

No. Leave it at the curb, in the driveway or in an open garage and text us a photo. We send a photo back when it is gone, and you pay by card over the phone.

Q/04 Permits

Do I need a permit to tear down a shed in Miami?

Sometimes. Larger sheds and anything wired or plumbed often need a demolition permit from your city or Miami-Dade County. We check with the building department, pull the permit when it is needed, and put the fee in the quote.
