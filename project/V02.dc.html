What do I do first when a pipe bursts?

Shut off the main valve, usually at the meter near the street. Turn off power to wet rooms if it is safe. Then call (954) 555-0122.

Does homeowners insurance cover water damage cleanup?

Sudden leaks, like a burst supply line, are often covered. Slow leaks and flood water usually are not. We document everything so your adjuster can decide fast.
