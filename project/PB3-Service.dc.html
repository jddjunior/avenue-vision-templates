6 Signs Your Tampa Panel Is Due for an Upgrade

Seeing two or more? We check the panel and tell you plainly: upgrade or repair.

Book My Check

Breakers trip when the AC kicks on

One trip is the breaker doing its job. Trips every July afternoon mean a maxed-out panel.

Lights dim when big things start

A dip when the AC, dryer or pool pump starts points to an undersized service or a loose main.

The panel is warm, buzzes or smells hot

Heat or a burnt smell means a failing breaker or bus bar. Call us, day or night.

Your insurer flagged the panel

Many Florida insurers flag Federal Pacific, Zinsco, Challenger and fuse panels at renewal.

You are adding an EV, pool heat or a room

A Level 2 charger alone can need a 40 to 60 amp breaker a 100A panel cannot spare.

Rust, double taps or no open slots

Salt air rusts panels from the inside. Two wires on one breaker, or no slots, mean it is time.
