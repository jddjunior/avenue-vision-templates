Chapter 3 · The Shower That Ran Cold

Why the hot water runs out before you do.

Much of Florida's water is hard: it carries minerals picked up from limestone. Inside your tank they settle as a sandy layer (3). The burner (4) has to heat through it, so you get less hot water and a popping sound. Meanwhile the anode rod (2) slowly gives itself up so the steel tank doesn't rust. When the rod is gone, the tank is next.

  • Popping, rumbling, lukewarm: we flush the scale out, about 45 minutes.
  • Rusty or smelly hot water: we pull the anode rod and show it to you before swapping it.
  • Water on the floor around the tank: the tank itself is leaking. That one gets replaced, usually the same or next day.

Glossary

My first plumbing words.

Five words that make any plumber's visit easier to follow. Learn the third one today; it can save a ceiling.

P-trap

say it: pee-trap

The U-bend under your sink. It holds a little water so sewer smell stays in the pipe.

Shutoff valve

say it: shut-off

The little handle behind a toilet or under a sink. Turn it clockwise to stop water to that one fixture.

Main shutoff

say it: the big one

Where water enters your house, often near the meter or the garage. It stops water to everything.

Flapper

say it: flap-er

The rubber lid at the bottom of the toilet tank. When it wears out, the toilet runs all day.

Cleanout

say it: clean-out

A capped pipe in your yard. It lets us reach a sewer clog without opening a wall or floor.

Why does my toilet keep running?

Usually it's the flapper, the rubber seal at the bottom of the tank. Hard water wears it out in a few years and water slips past it all day. Swapping it takes about 20 minutes, from $[PRICE] with the part. If the fill valve is also worn, we'll show you before replacing it.

Is a slow drain an emergency?

One slow sink usually isn't; it's often hair and soap near the top of the pipe. Two or more slow drains at once, or water rising in the tub when you flush, points to the main sewer line. Call that one in today, and go easy on water until we arrive.
