The Clearday Crew Promise

We hire for character first and teach the ladder work second. Every crew member signs this on day one, and you can hold them to it at your door.

  1. 01

    Early, not late

    We arrive inside the window you picked and text when we are on the way. If a job runs long, you hear it from us before your window opens, not after.

  2. 02

    Do what we said

    Your quote lists every pane, wall and add-on. That is the job we do and the price you pay. Nothing new shows up on the invoice at the door.

  3. 03

    Leave it better

    Before the van leaves, the crew lead walks the job with you. Missed a streak? We fix it on the spot, or come back within [DAYS] days at no charge.
